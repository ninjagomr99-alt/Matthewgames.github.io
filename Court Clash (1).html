Court Clash

Pick a court and a class, then a opponent. Baskets count 2, or 3 from beyond the arc. Half court is 1v1 to 11 with the clear-it rule; full court is 3v3 to 15.

P1: WASD move, Shift sprint, hold Space to charge a shot and release in the green zone, F steal or jump (block, rebound), Q pass (3v3), R epic dribble, E steal on the ground (vs CPU only).P2: arrows, Right Shift, Enter, / steal or jump, comma pass, period epic dribble.
